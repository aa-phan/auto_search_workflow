## Discretion choices made here

- Cutoffs live as pipeline constants with environment overrides (`POLL_CUTOFF_MINUTES`, `EVAL_CUTOFF_MINUTES`) rather than Settings rows: they are operator tuning knobs read from `Runs` phase times, not owner settings.
- Alert cutoff 22 minutes (CONTEXT leaves about 5 minutes for writes, alerts and the dashboard; GitHub's checkout / setup-python / pip steps of about a minute precede process start). A run whose alerts were cut is `degraded` (reason `alert cutoff`): a fresh alert waiting an hour deserves the owner's attention.

## Flagged assumptions (probe-surfaced, unresolved)

- EDGE MEAS-04 (unclassified, unresolved): process start is the anchor, but GitHub's setup steps (checkout, setup-python, pip install) run before Python starts and are not measured; the real tail after the 22-minute alert cutoff is therefore about 2 minutes, not 3. The live p95 of `Duration_S` (plan 02-07) is the check; tune `EVAL_CUTOFF_MINUTES` down if runs approach 24 minutes. The dashboard refresh after `run_cycle` is not cut (it is contained and reads about 10 s of state).
