## Discretion choices made here

- Folded counts are fed as one representative `JobFacts` per (run, dimension) with a weight, not one synthetic object per job (about 190,000 objects avoided). Its key is synthetic (`<ats>|~tally|<run_id>@<run_at>|<dim>`) and it is never put in `facts`. A known publication date is represented by a fixed sentinel instant (`1970-01-01T00:00:00Z`) that only feeds `date_known`.
- Presentation-only summary keys (the `storage` section added by plan 03-04) are excluded from the figures comparison; everything else in the summary is compared.
- Pitfall 9 (a hand-added tracker row for a job that is only in Identity) is a declared class, removed from both sides before comparing, so it cannot flap the clock.
