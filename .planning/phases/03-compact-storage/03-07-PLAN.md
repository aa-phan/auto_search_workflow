## Discretion choices made here (recorded in D-57)

- First fold on the first poller run of the UTC day after the cutover day (not in the cutover's own UTC day): the restore stays possible for the rest of that day (P3-06).
- At most once per UTC day, after the alert stage and the check; 20-minute process-clock cutoff; 500 `deleteDimension` requests per batch call (research A5 makes it a constant).
- "Daily growth in cells" = median over complete UTC days of the change in allocated grid cells, computed from `Runs.Events_Rows` times 7 for Events plus the Identity and Tally row growth since the cutover (5 and 4 cells a row); the before figure comes from the cutover snapshot.
