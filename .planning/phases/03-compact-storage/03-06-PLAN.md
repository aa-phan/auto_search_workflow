<objective>
Build the owner-triggered cutover (copy the retained rows, verify, swap the tabs atomically) and the emergency restore that makes P3-06's "reversible until the first fold" true, with their workflow inputs.

Purpose: STOR-01, ROADMAP success criterion 3 (the swap itself; the fold and the before/after report follow in plan 03-07). Decisions P3-06, P3-08, P3-14.

Flagged refinement of P3-06 (recorded in D-57 by Task 3): renaming `Events_Legacy` back by hand would lose every Events row written after the cutover, so jobs first seen after it (pending, evaluated, alerted) would look new and could be alerted again. The restore therefore appends the post-cutover rows to Events_Legacy before the rename pair, and is refused once a fold has deleted anything. This adds a command and an input that CONTEXT did not name; it changes nothing unless the owner dispatches it.
Output: cutover and restore commands, Sheet and CSV tab operations, mode handling in the pipeline, workflow inputs, tests, docs.

## Discretion choices made here (recorded in D-57 by Task 3)

- Exit codes for both commands: 0 done (or a dry-run plan whose gate passes), 1 refused by the gate, 2 error. Both run under the `poller` lease in the Job Hunter workflow's 25-minute window.
- The copy is verified twice: read-back count plus checksum, then parity on the would-be state. A failure leaves `Events` untouched and `Events_New` in place for the next attempt (which replaces it).
- `Events_Legacy` also gets the warning-only protection (research Open Question 4): it is the restore source.
- `restored` is a terminal safety mode: dual writing without a clock; compacting again needs a new owner decision (and a new seed of the clock).
